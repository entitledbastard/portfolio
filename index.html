About me

I started at Profectus Group in a data role. I learned everything I could about data and database management, ended up owning part of the data funnel, and then used my UX skills to make that work easier. My background is industrial design (OCAD University).

The thing I care about most in an interface: when you land on it, it should be clear what it's for and how to use it.

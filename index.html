How I work

  1. Understand the problem

    I talk to the people who'll use it, watch how they work now, and run workshops or card sorts with the team.

  2. Map the flow

    From the first step to the last.

  3. Sketch on paper

    Then turn the sketches into rough mockups.

  4. Test and refine

    When time and resources allow, I get the mockups in front of users. Then I refine over several rounds until the interface is clear and works.

  5. Design system, then build

    I set up the design system and foundations in Figma. That makes the full interface faster to build, and it gives AI a system to work from. From there I use AI to build working interfaces for another round of testing, then the full app, and I keep refining.

Where AI fits

I run the research myself. It's conversations, workshops and watching people work, and I want to be part of all of it. AI helps me organise what I find and spot patterns I might have missed.

Once the structure and flows are solid, AI works from my Figma design system. I use it for the detail work that takes hours by hand: tuning corner radii, refining the colour palette, adjusting spacing. It's also how I explore. I can try variations or change the visual direction, and the whole interface follows without starting over.
